# User Manual

## Purpose

SpanishCafe lets learners practice guided Spanish conversations in a virtual café/restaurant.

## Using the simulation

1. Launch the complete Unity project or its playable build.
2. Enter the café environment.
3. Use the available movement and camera controls to approach a character.
4. Select the character to open a conversation.
5. Read the Spanish dialogue.
6. Choose one of the available responses to continue the exchange.
7. Continue until the conversation ends, then explore or select another character.

The developer confirms that the current navigation and conversation features work. Exact keyboard bindings and on-screen labels are not specified here because they were not established by the supplied archives.

## Learning experience

The guided conversations provide opportunities to practice comprehension and everyday restaurant-related Spanish expressions.

## Audio

The scene includes café music and ambient chatter. Check the application and device volume if you cannot hear them.
